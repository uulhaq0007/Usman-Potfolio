A navy-and-gold law firm site across eleven routes, including per-attorney and per-practice-area detail pages. The emblem is drawn in code and the caseload donut is SVG built from stroke-dasharray segments sized by share of total. Unknown slugs render their own not-found states rather than falling through to the global 404.
