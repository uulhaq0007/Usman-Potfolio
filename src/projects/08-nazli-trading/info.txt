Interface design for a Japanese used-vehicle export business — sourcing at auction in
Japan and shipping to buyers in Bangladesh, Malaysia and the UK. Covers the purchase
ledger, a consolidated shipment report, letter-of-credit entry and three separate sales
channels, built around dense tables carrying chassis numbers, auction houses, ports of
loading and discharge, and yen totals. Design only — no build.
