A guided builder that turns a child into the hero of their own printed storybook. Walks
through choosing a character and hairstyle, uploading a reference photo with clear
guidance on what works, entering the child's details, then confirming the order while a
progress screen renders the cover. Includes an admin panel for tracking orders through
personalisation, address check and print-ready. Built in vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript
with no framework.
