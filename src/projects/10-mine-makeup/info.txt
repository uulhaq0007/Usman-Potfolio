A multi-seller beauty marketplace where independent brands list their own products.
React and Tailwind on the front, Express and MongoDB behind it, with JWT auth splitting
shoppers from sellers — buyers get browsing, cart and order history, sellers get a
dashboard for listing and stock. Includes category filtering, search, sorting, sold-out
handling and protected routes that redirect unauthenticated visitors to sign-in.
