category: Dashboard App

A management portal for dance studios, built in TypeScript on Radix primitives with
TanStack Query handling the data layer. The overview surfaces follower growth, review
distribution, audience by city and dance style, and event volume month on month. Beyond
it sit an events manager with calendar, RSVPs, photos and updates, and a paginated
reviews feed. Includes OAuth callback handling, protected routing and a studio switcher
for owners running more than one location.
