A donation and volunteer platform for a welfare NGO, running entirely on local
infrastructure — own MongoDB, uploads written to disk, no cloud service contacted at
runtime. Donors submit payment screenshots that an admin verifies before they count
toward totals; volunteers apply through a three-step form; a Zakat calculator works out
nisab server-side from configurable gold and silver rates. The six-tab admin panel covers
donations, volunteers, messages, site settings and homepage content. Every page ships in
English and Urdu with full RTL and a Nastaliq face, plus light and dark themes.
